How raw milk works in Ireland.

The sale of raw cows’ milk was banned in Ireland in 1996. EU hygiene rules later let each country regulate it instead, and Irish regulations introduced in 2015 and guidance agreed with the Department of Agriculture in 2018 now set the conditions for producers.

A farm selling raw milk outside the small-scale exemption must be approved or registered with the Department of Agriculture, Food and the Marine (DAFM) and label the milk with a health advisory. The Food Safety Authority of Ireland (FSAI) still advises against drinking it, and calls raw milk high-risk.

Before you buy, ask the seller whether the farm is approved or registered with DAFM, and read the FSAI’s advice on raw milk, including who should avoid it.
