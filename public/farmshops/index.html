More than a list of farm shops.

Farm shops sell food straight from the farm: eggs, meat, vegetables, dairy and more. Buying there keeps money with small producers and tells you where your food comes from.

This finder combines open map data with the official register of raw drinking milk producers, so you can search one place for both.

How the data is built. Farm shops are mapped by volunteers on OpenStreetMap, so coverage is uneven and some shops are missing or have closed. The list is rebuilt weekly.

Looking for raw milk? Use the raw milk finder and read the rules first.

Check before you travel. Details come from volunteers and public registers and can be out of date. Phone the shop for opening hours and stock.

Farm shop and milk vending machine data: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Raw milk producers: Food Standards Agency register, published under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Registered farms: Food Standards Agency Food Hygiene Rating Scheme (only farms with a public address; the FSA withholds home addresses). Postcodes and regions: postcodes.io.
